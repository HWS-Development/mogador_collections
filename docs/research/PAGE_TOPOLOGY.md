# Page Topology

## Global Shell
1. Fixed transparent/compact header.
2. Fullscreen editorial menu overlay.
3. Main content route.
4. Conversion dock / booking affordance.
5. Prefooter newsletter/conversion block.
6. Footer directory.

## Home
1. Radical cinematic hero with booking.
2. Direct booking proof strip.
3. Brand intro/services.
4. Intent grid.
5. Destination constellation.
6. Numbered storytelling chapters.
7. Signature hotels carousel/grid area.
8. Destinations directory.
9. Brand moments.
10. Numbers.
11. Final booking CTA.

## Interior Pages
1. `PageHero` cinematic opener.
2. `SignatureExperience` editorial split.
3. Page-specific content sections.
4. Gallery/proof sections where available.
5. Final CTA or direct proof.

## Hotel Detail
1. `PageHero` with booking.
2. `SignatureExperience` personalized to hotel.
3. Intro facts.
4. Booking panel.
5. Immersive gallery.
6. Room cinematic selector.
7. Scroll overlay moments.
8. Service matrix.
9. Official sections.
10. Final gallery and booking CTA.

## Interaction Models
- Hero: load + scroll + pointer reactive.
- Menu: click-driven open/close, hover-driven preview.
- Booking: form submit time-driven pending/ready.
- Galleries: time-driven autoplay + click arrows.
- Destination filters/room tabs: click-driven state switch.
- Cards: hover-driven 3D/object reveal.
